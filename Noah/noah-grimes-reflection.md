## What Would I Do Differently?

If I were to do this project again, I would try to integrate the electronics more directly into the box. Binary display LEDs and RGB LED would be just be poking out the side of the box, would look cleaner, and they would probably have soldered connections, so less likely to get randomly unplugged, we had some issues with this. This would also include mounting the Arduino and Pi internally, just clean stuff up a bit more. I would also backlight the ramp, as this gave us much better results when we were detecting widgets in the classroom. But honestly, I'm just happy that this thing worked well enough that the only thing I feel like we could hugely improve on was backlighting the ramp and ✨aesthetics✨
