# Noah Grimes' Project Reflection

## What did I do on the project?

I did the majority of the CAD for this project. I was also supposed to do the Python code, but I never really had time to write it, we ended up just using the original placeholder genAI code.

## What Challenges Did I Overcome?

Tons of things, such as the side walls being too big to laser cut, having to redo finger joints constantly, webcam being too wide to fit between side walls, and many, many more issues. Fixing the horrible AI code was also a pain, once I had to kidnap Keegan to help fix it with me during seminar.

## What Would I Do Differently?

If I were to do this project again, I would try to integrate the electronics more directly into the box. Binary display LEDs and RGB LED would be just be poking out the side of the box, would look cleaner, and they would probably have soldered connections, so less likely to get randomly unplugged, we had some issues with this. This would also include mounting the Arduino and Pi internally, just clean stuff up a bit more. I would also backlight the ramp, as this gave us much better results when we were detecting widgets in the classroom. But honestly, I'm just happy that this thing worked well enough that the only things I feel like we could've done differently to make it better were backlighting the ramp and ✨_aesthetics_✨

By project one I'll assume you mean the useless box, since the proximity alarm project wasn't really a full-on project.
